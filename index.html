Common Questions

Mortgage FAQs for Lethbridge clients

What does a mortgage broker in Lethbridge do?+
A mortgage broker shops multiple lenders on your behalf to find competitive mortgage rates and terms, then guides you through the process from application to approval. Instead of being tied to one bank, you get access to a wide network of lenders.
How much does it cost to use Marty as my broker?+
For most residential mortgages there's no cost to you — the lender compensates the broker. That means you get access to multiple lenders and rates without paying a fee. Call 403.308.4439 to confirm the details for your situation.
